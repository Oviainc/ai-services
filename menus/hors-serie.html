The Solstice Table — a menu out of the series, written for a single occasion: the longest night of the year, and the light that turns after it. Twelve dishes, one table, served once — by reservation only.

STARTERS

The Long Dark

Glazed Beets · Smoked Cream · Rye Crumb

Beets roasted until their sugars turn to glaze, under a whisper of smoked cream. The dish stands for latent space — everything a system holds unseen, arranged in the dark, waiting to be called into form.

What the dark keeps.

Ember & Frost

Seared Scallops · Grated Horseradish · Brown Butter

Heat first, then frost: scallops seared hard and finished under horseradish grated fine as snow. Annealing, plated — a material heated, then cooled slowly until its structure sets stronger than the first.

Cooled into shape.

Night Orchard

Compressed Apple · Walnut · Celery Leaf · Smoked Honey

The orchard, pressed and kept: apple compressed until its flavour doubles, walnut and celery leaf for the green note, honey smoked for depth. Compression as a course — a whole season stored in a fraction of its size, with nothing essential lost.

The orchard, kept.

MAINS

Stored Light

Slow-Roasted Goose · Parsnip Silk · Preserved Cherries

Goose roasted low and long, cherries kept from the summer at their peak. A checkpoint, served: the season saved at its best moment, restored whole when the dark months ask for it.

Saved at the peak.

The Returning Sun

Turbot · Saffron Beurre Blanc · Fennel

White fish under a sauce the colour of late light. Emergence at the table — brightness arriving gradually, each element simple, the whole more than any part predicted.

Light, arriving slowly.

The Slow Compound

Ember-Glazed Short Rib · Charred Onion · Bone-Marrow Crumb

Short rib glazed over embers, hour after hour, until small additions become a crust no single hour could make. Compounding, served as a main: modest inputs, given time, returning far more than they cost.

Time does the work.

DESSERTS

Solstice Torte

Dark Chocolate · Chestnut Cream · Gold Leaf

The year, reduced to a slice: chocolate dark as the longest night, chestnut at its heart, one fleck of gold for the turn. Distillation as dessert — everything kept, nothing wasted.

Pairs with: First Light

The year, reduced.

The State Change

Torched Meringue · Frozen Core · Smoked Caramel

Fire outside, frost inside, eaten in the moment between the two. A phase transition on a plate: the same substance in two states at once, and all the energy living at the boundary.

Pairs with: The Thermal Store

Two states at once.

Recalled Summer

Preserved Peach · Almond Cream · Verbena

A peach put up in July, opened in the dark of the year, tasting of both. Retrieval as dessert: memory stored whole, recalled on demand, unchanged by the months between.

Pairs with: The Clarified Night

Summer, recalled.

DRINKS

First Light

Clarified Citrus · Juniper · Sparkling

Clear as morning, poured last and meant first: the drink is a forecast — citrus clarified until it shines, juniper underneath, and the sparkle of a turn everyone at the table is waiting for.

Pairs with: Solstice Torte

The turn, foretold.

The Thermal Store

Warm Pear · Ginger · Star Anise

Heat, banked in pear and spice and spent slowly across the table. A thermal store in a glass: warmth taken in while it is plentiful, released when the night is longest.

Pairs with: The State Change

Heat, kept for later.

The Clarified Night

Clarified Dark Tea · Blackberry · Smoke

A dark drink made clear: tea and blackberry clarified until the colour gives up its secrets and only the flavour stays dark. Clarification as a nightcap — complexity, filtered to its essence.

Pairs with: Recalled Summer

Dark, made clear.

RESERVE

These menus are cooked to order — for a private table, a studio evening, or the full season brought home. Places are few, and the season is short.
