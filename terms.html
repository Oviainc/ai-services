The short version

Nothing is sold on this website. Every engagement begins with a consultation and a written plan — scope, timeline and fixed price, agreed before work starts. The plan, signed, is the agreement for that engagement; these terms are the general frame around it. Fees are quoted in the plan and nowhere else. Client data is governed by the Privacy & AI Transparency page, which forms part of these terms.

1. Who these terms govern

Ovia Inc. (“OVIA”) provides the services described on this site. These terms apply to visitors of the site and to clients. Where a signed written plan says something different from these general terms, the plan governs that engagement.

2. The consultation

A consultation is a working session, not a sales call. It produces a written plan: what will be done, how long it will take, and what it will cost, at a fixed price. The plan is yours to keep whether or not the work proceeds, and a consultation creates no obligation on either side. Every serious enquiry receives a reply within one business day.

3. Engagements and the written plan

Work starts only when a written plan has been accepted. Changes to scope, timeline or price are agreed in writing before they are made — there are no surprise additions and no bills that grow on their own. Each engagement has a named person responsible for the work, and the people you meet are the people who do it.

4. Fees and payment

Fees are quoted per engagement, in the written plan. No prices are published on this site, and no fee is owed for work that is not in an accepted plan. Unless the plan states otherwise, invoices are due on receipt.

5. Timelines

Delivery dates live in the written plan. If a date is at risk, the client is told before the date passes, with the reason and the revised date — not after.

6. Client materials, data and deliverables

Material and data a client provides remain the client's. What OVIA builds for an engagement — the deliverables named in the plan, including source files where the plan lists them — is handed over as the plan states. How data and AI tools are handled, including which providers may process what, is set out in full on the Privacy & AI Transparency page; a client may restrict processing in writing at any time.

7. Intellectual property

On payment in full, the client may use the engagement's deliverables for their business without further fee. OVIA retains its general methods, tools and know-how, which it develops across engagements. OVIA never publishes a client's identity, material or results without that client's written consent.

8. Honest limits

Work is performed with reasonable care by senior people, and AI-assisted output is reviewed by a person before it is delivered. OVIA does not guarantee specific business outcomes — revenue, bookings, rankings or call volumes depend on factors outside any provider's control. To the extent permitted by law, OVIA's liability for an engagement is limited to the fees paid for that engagement.

9. Ending an engagement

Either side may end an engagement in writing. Work completed to that date is payable as the plan states, and completed deliverables are handed over. Sections on data, intellectual property and liability survive the end of an engagement.

10. These terms

These terms may be updated; the version in force is the one published on this page, identified by the reference at the bottom of the page. OVIA works with clients internationally; the terms agreed for a specific engagement are recorded in its written plan. Questions about these terms are answered plainly and in writing — ask through the contact form on the homepage.
