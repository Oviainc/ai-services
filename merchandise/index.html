A black-and-white collection in the spirit of the nineties houses — baroque weight, nothing in colour, nothing synthetic. Natural blends only: cotton, hemp, linen, wool, leather. Every garment in a choice of length — Long, Regular, Short.

The Crest

The laurel wreath and the crown, the OVIA mark alone at the centre — no frame, no octagon — and the four Latin words on the banner beneath. One colour only: black on white, white on black.

The Words

Ordo — Order · Valentia — Strength · Intelligentia — Intelligence · Artificialis — Craft

The house values, in Latin, carried on the crest banner — the same four words that stand on the main site. The clothes carry the logo alone, no crest.
