The Manifesto

Ten maxims. Everything published under this name answers to them — every engagement, every page, every piece of work that leaves the studio.

  1. 1

    OVIA is a studio and a lab. The studio designs. The lab proves. One senior team does both.

  2. 2

    The business is the run. Building is the beginning; the running is the deliverable.

  3. 3

    Nothing is finished at launch.

  4. 4

    Never hand over and leave. The team that builds it runs it.

  5. 5

    Everything in writing. Fixed price. A named person responsible. Every serious inquiry answered within one business day.

  6. 6

    Every client conversation in the client's own language. Multilingual is the standard, never a feature.

  7. 7

    Premium is a standard of work, not a claim: senior hands only, no filler, nothing generic, nothing that could be mistaken for another firm's work.

  8. 8

    Measure everything that runs. What gets measured gets better.

  9. 9

    The library compounds. Every deployment sharpens the next; the authority is built to outlast any single project.

  10. 10

    Any company. Any domain. The operation, run end to end — to your satisfaction.
