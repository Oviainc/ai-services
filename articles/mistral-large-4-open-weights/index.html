Open Weights, Open Operations: Mistral Large 4

The release

On Thursday, October 29, 2026, Mistral released the weights of Mistral Large 4, a natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model of one trillion parameters, with roughly 49 billion parameters active on each token. The weights are published on Hugging Face, free to download and run. By Friday the release was the top AI story on Hacker News with 1,472 points — a measure of how closely the developer world watches the open-weights frontier.

Why open weights change the operating picture

A downloaded model is an asset, not a service. It runs where the team runs it, keeps working if a vendor changes strategy, and can be inspected, fine-tuned and frozen at a known version for regulated work. Trained in Europe and framed by its maker as sovereign infrastructure, Large 4 also lands in a policy moment: governments and enterprises increasingly ask where a model lives and who can switch it off. Open weights answer both questions in one move — it lives here, and nobody can.

The economics move in-house

Mistral reports that Large 4 ties a leading competitor on a coding-agent index while using 47% fewer tokens per task, with an API price of $0.70 per million input tokens and $2.10 per million output tokens for those who prefer the hosted route. Those performance figures are vendor-reported and await independent validation. The structural point stands regardless: when the weights are free, the differentiator becomes operations — quantisation choices, serving stacks, batching, caching and evaluation. Cost per correct answer becomes a craft metric, and craft can be learned, certified and improved.

The fine print

Open weights are not the same as an unrestricted licence. Mistral's terms allow broad use of the weights, while commercial redistribution of the model as a hosted service requires a separate licence. Teams planning products on top of Large 4 should read the licence before designing the business model, not after. Provenance matters too: a trillion-parameter download deserves the same supply-chain discipline as any other critical dependency — verified sources, pinned versions, tested updates.

What to watch

Three signals will show whether Large 4 matters beyond a headline week: independent benchmark replication of the efficiency claims; the first wave of fine-tunes and agent frameworks adopting it as a default open option; and enterprise deployments that choose it for sovereignty reasons rather than price. At Ovia, open models are tracked the way operators track weather — what changed, what running the model costs, and what the model unlocks for agents in production.
