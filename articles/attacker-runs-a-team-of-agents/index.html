The Attacker Now Runs a Team of Agents, Not a Keyboard

The report

On October 29, 2026, CrowdStrike published its account of a global espionage campaign attributed — on CrowdStrike's assessment — to VAPOR PANDA, a China-nexus adversary tracked since 2021. Beginning October 17, a single controller running the ARTEX agentic framework was directed against more than 200 organisations in at least 14 countries over an 11-day window: foreign ministries, a European country's presidency and prime minister's office, defence and intelligence bodies, semiconductor and pharmaceutical firms, think tanks and telecommunications providers. CrowdStrike confirmed post-exploitation activity at nine organisations; the campaign was still active at publication, and the attribution and target counts are CrowdStrike's, reported here as such.

Inside ARTEX

The architecture will look familiar to anyone building legitimate agent systems, and that familiarity is the point. ARTEX coordinates a main agent with subordinate planner and worker agents over a shared graph of facts, findings and goals, advancing each engagement from initial access through host reconnaissance to lateral movement. A human operator stays in the loop, intervening at decision points; recovered transcripts show the operator directing agents to write web shells, extract domain administrator passwords and inventory endpoint sensor coverage. Multiple engagements ran in parallel against different targets, each tracked as its own task — a small team, multiplied by software.

Scale, in numbers

Two figures measure the shift. In a single intrusion, the planning agents consumed on the order of 1.4 billion input tokens, CrowdStrike reports — a scale of automated reasoning no keyboard team sustains. And the tempo: hands-on-keyboard activity tracked over fifteen years typically ran at up to about 40 commands per hour; with ARTEX, that pace could exceed 1,500 commands per hour, CrowdStrike's Adam Meyers told CyberScoop, with requests arriving as often as every ten seconds. CrowdStrike also counted agentic adversary attacks rising from roughly 45 in September to 126 in October. The tradecraft is established; the speed and parallelism are new.

The counter-design

Defence has to answer in kind — organised, observable and fast — and the counter-design is already visible in the same architecture, inverted. Least privilege shrinks what any single agent, account or session can reach. Expiring credentials close the window that stolen access depends on. Observable state — shared graphs of facts and findings, maintained for defenders — turns detection into a race between two teams of agents rather than a watch kept by tired eyes alone. The Staywired Technology Coordinator years taught the perimeter version of that lesson; NVIDIA-Certified Professional: AI Operations teaches the agent version: instrument the system, bound the authority, rehearse the response.

What to watch

CrowdStrike assesses that agentic frameworks will keep spreading among state-nexus adversaries, and the same ARTEX tooling has already appeared under separate attribution elsewhere — a sign of a maturing ecosystem rather than a single campaign. Watch for defender-side agent teams moving from conference talks into standard practice, for token-scale telemetry becoming a detection signal in its own right, and for identity controls — short-lived credentials, scoped sessions — becoming the control plane where these contests are actually decided.

The same lesson applies to the budget conversation. A planning layer that can spend on the order of 1.4 billion input tokens on one intrusion changes the economics of attack in a way defenders must price honestly: automated reasoning at that scale is cheap for the side that runs it and expensive for the side that answers it by hand. Matching the tempo does not require matching the recklessness — defender agents operate inside known estates, with ground truth an attacker has to discover — but the tempo itself is no longer optional. Operations centres will staff agent fleets the way earlier generations staffed watch rotations.
